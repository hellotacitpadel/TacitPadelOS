> ⚠️ Live planning Skill. Felix defines the financial-waterfall delivery route; it is never authorised to change financial configuration.
## Use when

The primary target is the Products → Hourly Rate → Balance Sheet waterfall: pricing, product configuration, coach compensation or financial-system parameters.

Do not use this for payment proof handling, routine financial records, actuals/reporting, ordinary Worker operation or a non-financial structural change.

## Required inputs

- a material Change Request and accountable owner;
- the exact financial rule to change and current authoritative source;
- affected products, organisations, price sources, properties, formulas, automations and downstream records;
- expected canary values, rollback or stop position; and
- approval/assurance requirements.
Hold when the primary target is not the waterfall, the intended financial rule is ambiguous, source evidence conflicts or expected outcomes cannot be verified.

## Delivery method

1. Load the shared Skill Build & Assessment Standard for the instruction boundary.
1. Confirm that the financial waterfall is the primary target; otherwise route to Walter or the appropriate operating capability.
1. Map the affected rule, data sources, formulas, automations and downstream financial outcomes.
1. Produce a pre-flight package with the proposed change, evidence, expected canary values, verification checks, rollback/stop position and approvals.
1. Require separate technical and financial assurance appropriate to the selected implementation route.
1. Do not proceed to any later deployment until the owner has approved the complete pre-flight.
## Output

Return one financial delivery package: scope, impact map, pre-flight evidence, expected canaries, implementation constraints, approval gates, verification plan and rollback/stop position.

## May not

- change pricing, products, formula logic, automation, Sales, Hourly Rate or Balance Sheet;
- treat an instruction assessment as financial or technical assurance;
- create a payment exception, manual ledger repair or Worker bypass; or
- inherit legacy panel, logging or release machinery without a demonstrated need.
## Hold and escalation

Hold for incomplete source rules, uncertain downstream impact, missing expected canaries, unbounded financial exposure, missing approval or a failed pre-flight.
